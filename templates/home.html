Food waste is a global crisis

Traditional manual inspection is slow, inconsistent, and error-prone. This project proposes an AI-driven alternative: instant, accurate, scalable freshness detection from any camera.

✨ Key Features

Everything in one scan

From classification to nutrition, our AI pipeline delivers complete produce intelligence in seconds.

🎯

Zero-Shot Freshness Classification

Using OpenCLIP's multimodal embeddings, the system classifies produce without any domain-specific training. Simply describe the item — the model understands it.

🔬

Surface Defect Detection

HSV color space analysis identifies blemishes, rot patches, and abnormal discoloration. Results are expressed as a percentage defect score with visual feedback.

🧪

Nutritional Insight Engine

A curated nutrition database maps detected produce to vitamins, minerals, and health benefits — making every scan an educational moment.

🤖

Unknown Object Handling

When confidence is low, the system gracefully flags the item as "Unknown" rather than forcing a wrong classification — a key safety feature for real-world deployment.

⚙️ System Pipeline

From photo to insight

A six-stage AI pipeline that transforms a raw image into comprehensive produce intelligence.

1

Image Upload

User uploads a photo via the web interface. Accepted formats: JPG, PNG. Drag & drop supported.

2

Preprocessing

The image is resized, normalized, and converted to a tensor compatible with CLIP's visual encoder.

3

CLIP Classification

OpenCLIP computes cosine similarity between the image embedding and 50+ produce text prompts. Top match + confidence returned.

4

HSV Defect Detection

Converted to HSV, defect pixels (brown, dark, unusual hue) are masked and scored as a percentage of total surface area.

5

Freshness Estimation

A weighted formula combining defect score and CLIP confidence produces a 0–100% freshness score and a verdict: Fresh, Caution, or Non-Consumable.

6

Nutrition & AI Insights

Matched produce is looked up in the nutrition database. AI generates storage tips, health notes, and verdict reasoning for display.
