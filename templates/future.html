🚚 Broader Impact

AI Transforming the Food Supply Chain

The impact potential of AI-powered freshness detection scales far beyond a single scanner app.

By flagging borderline produce before it enters distribution, AI systems can redirect items to faster-moving channels, reducing the time between detection and consumption — and preventing loss that would otherwise occur silently in cold storage.
Real-time freshness scores can feed into dynamic pricing systems. Produce nearing end-of-life could be automatically discounted rather than discarded, benefitting consumers and reducing waste simultaneously.
Regulatory agencies require produce to meet minimum freshness and safety standards. An automated AI audit trail provides timestamps, scores, and images for every batch — far superior to spot-check manual inspection logs.
A consumer-facing app lets shoppers make better purchasing decisions at the market, reduce household food waste, and understand the nutritional value of what they buy — bridging AI innovation and everyday life.
💡

Open Source Potential

An open-source release of the model weights and API would enable researchers, NGOs, and smallholder farmers in developing regions to deploy freshness detection at zero cost.
